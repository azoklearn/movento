# Prompt: HOLLOWMERE, "A Night of Lanterns": Halloween descent scroll

Give me the superdetailed exact prompt to recreate this page exactly as it is, including the correct asset URL, all elements, animations, and fonts. This is that prompt.

## What it is

Build a single-page site for a fictional Halloween night festival called **Hollowmere**. Use plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript in one file, with no libraries.

**The structure.** The page is **one continuous illustrated descent**: five full-bleed flat-vector Halloween scenes stacked vertically into a single tall picture.

| # | Scene |
|---|---|
| 1 | A moonlit canyon of gothic houses with a castle on the moon |
| 2 | An underground crypt laboratory with a glass tower of captured ghosts |
| 3 | A mill chamber with a giant jack-o'-lantern-studded wheel |
| 4 | A cauldron foundry pouring molten potion |
| 5 | A cave mouth opening onto a moonlit lake of floating lanterns |

**The joins.** Between every pair of scenes lies an illustrated band of rock strata: the ground of the scene above on top, the cave ceiling of the scene below underneath. The scenes therefore join through real drawn geology, never through gradient fades.

**The art.** Every scene is drawn in one art style: angular shard-cut shapes, grainy speckled texture, glowing rim light, deep indigo, violet and electric blue with pale cyan ghost-light and warm orange jack-o'-lantern accents. A **pale cyan "ghost-light" waterfall** runs from the hero's pool down through the scenes, like the falls in an illustrated-scroll corporate site.

**The motion is all code:**
- The falls flow continuously as canvas streaks.
- Lava streams pour.
- Mist breathes and lanterns and jack-o'-lanterns flicker.
- Motes, spray, bubbles and moonlight shimmer drift on small canvases.
- Ghost sprites bob and shy away from the cursor, and bats flap across the skies.
- The page scrolls with eased momentum.
- The hero title rises letter by letter on load and drifts with parallax, and section copy rises line by line out of masks.
- A warm "lantern" glow follows the cursor.

## The seams: illustrated rock bands across every join

- **Band:** a `.band` box inside it, `position: absolute; left: 0; top: 0; width: 100vw; aspect-ratio: 2688 / 1152; transform: translateY(−50%)`, so it straddles the join exactly. It holds the transparent band image (`<img>`, filling the box, `z-index 1`) and, where the falls pass through, a falls canvas behind the image (`z-index 0`).

- **Mobile:** the band uses the lower scene's `--focus` with the same width and offset formula as `.art`, so its opening stays over the lower scene's falls.

## The falls renderer (`canvas.fall`)

**Element styling:**
- `mix-blend-mode: screen`, so the streaks add light on top of the painted falls.
- A **double CSS mask**, intersected (`mask-composite: intersect`, `-webkit-mask-composite: source-in`):
  - horizontal: transparent → black 22 % → black 78 % → transparent;
  - vertical: transparent → black 12 % → black 82 % → transparent.
- The streams therefore feather out at the sides, top and bottom.

**Streak sprites:** pre-render 8 × 160 offscreen canvases, each a vertical gradient, two per kind.

| Kind | Sprite A | Sprite B |
|---|---|---|
| ghost | `rgba(190,240,255,0)` → `rgba(210,248,255,.95)` at 35 % → `rgba(120,200,255,0)` | `rgba(120,190,255,0)` → `rgba(140,215,255,.8)` at 50 % → `rgba(90,150,255,0)` |
| lava | `rgba(255,190,80,0)` → `rgba(255,236,150,1)` at 40 % → `rgba(255,120,30,0)` | `rgba(255,120,30,0)` → `rgba(255,150,50,.9)` at 50 % → `rgba(255,80,20,0)` |

**Sizing (on load and resize):**
- Read `offsetWidth` and `offsetHeight`, which ignore transforms, so the hero's load zoom does not distort it.
- The pixel ratio is capped at 1.5.

**Streaks per canvas:**

| | ghost | lava |
|---|---|---|
| Count | `round(w / 2.2) × 2` | `round(w / 3) × 3` |
| x | Anywhere across the width | Within the middle 50 % |
| Stroke width | .8–4px | 1–3.2px |
| Length | 60–280px | 30–100px |
| Speed | 420–760 px/s × the canvas `data-speed` | 195–350 px/s × the canvas `data-speed` |
| Opacity | .18–.73 | .18–.73 |

Each streak gets a random phase and one of the two sprites (45 % / 55 %).

**Each frame:**
- Clear the canvas and switch to `globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter'`.
- For every streak, `y = ((t × v × speed + phase) mod (h + len)) − len`.
- Draw the sprite at (x, y) and again one span higher, so streaks wrap seamlessly.
- Ghost streaks dim towards the edges by `1 − (|x/w − .5| × 2)² × .6`.
- Only canvases whose rect is within 50px of the viewport are drawn.

## Particle canvases (`canvas.dust`)

**Setup:**
- Each canvas is full-art, with `mix-blend-mode: screen`.
- Each particle has u, v, r, phase and speed .4–1.2.
- An optional box (`data-x/w/y/h`, in %) limits where the particles live.
- A canvas is drawn only while it is on screen.

| Kind | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `wisp` (hero) | Rises at `.02 × speed` of the height per second from the canyon floor. It respawns at v .98 across x .2–.8 once v passes .35. It sways ±1.2 % and has radius .8–2.6px. Opacity fades in over the first .25 of its rise and pulses with `|sin(2t + phase)|`. Colour `rgb(190,240,255)` |
| `mote` (crypt) | Drifts up very slowly (.008 × speed), wraps top to bottom, sways ±2 %, radius .6–2px, opacity .25–.75, colour `rgb(170,225,255)` |
| `spray` (mill) | Lives a 1-second loop, `life = (t × speed × .8 + phase) mod 1`. It is thrown sideways by `(r − .5) × .6` of the box and falls in a parabola (`life² × .9` of the box height). Radius .7–2.3px, opacity 1 − life, colour `rgb(200,245,255)` |
| `bubble` (cauldron) | `life = (t × speed × .16 + phase) mod 1`. Rises from the bottom of the box to the top, wobbling `sin(1.4t)` × 3 % of the box, with radius (2–9px) × width/1440. Drawn as a radial gradient: `rgba(255,240,180,a)` → `rgba(255,170,70,.55a)` → transparent, with a highlight offset up and left. `a = sin(life × π) × .9` |

## Sprites in motion

**Ghosts:**
- **Placement:** each ghost is placed by `translate3d` at its % anchor in the art, centred, with drift added on top.
- **Wander:** `x += sin(.35t + phase) × 1.2 %` of the art width, `y += sin(.9t + phase) × 1.4 %` of the art height.
- **Sway:** rotation `sin(.7t + phase) × 6° + offsetX × .05`.
- **Cursor repulsion:** within 260px of the cursor, a ghost is pushed directly away by up to `(1 − d/260)^1.5 × 90` px. The push eases at `1 − e^(−3dt)`, so ghosts glide aside and drift back.

**Bats:**
- **Path:** each bat flies `x: −8 % → 108 %` of the art width over 9–16s, looping, with a random offset.
- **Height:** `y = y0 + sin(p × 9 + offset × 6) × (1–4) %`.
- **Size:** 1.6–3.2 % of the art width, aspect 203 / 221.
- **Flap:** wings-up and wings-down frames swap 8–12 times a second, toggled by a `.down` class.

## Motion system

### Smooth scroll

- Each frame: `cur += (scrollY − cur) × (1 − e^(−7.5·dt))`. It snaps when within .05px.
- `#scroller` gets `translate3d(0, −cur px, 0)`.
- Anchor links jump instantly by native scroll, and the easing does the glide.

### Hero parallax

- The hero `.art` translates `+cur × .32` px, so the illustration lags behind.
- `.hero-copy` translates `+cur × .55` px.
- The title's `--lsx` grows from 0 to .18em as the hero scrolls away. With `hp = clamp(cur / heroHeight, 0, 1)`, the letters spread apart as you descend.

### Load intro

All Web Animations:
- **Letters:** each letter rises `translateY(110%) → 0` over 1200ms, starting at 250ms, 70ms apart.
- **Subtitle:** over 1600ms from 900ms: letter-spacing 1.4em → .62em, `translateY(14px) → 0`, `clip-path: inset(0 50% 0 50%) → inset(0)`. It opens from the centre outwards.
- **Hero art:** `scale(1.09) → 1` over 2600ms, composited with "add" so it stacks on the parallax.
- **Nav:** slides down over 900ms from 500ms.
- **Easing:** `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)` throughout.

### Section reveals

- **Trigger:** the first time a copy block's top passes 80 % of the viewport height.
- **Lines:** each `.ln > span` rises `translateY(105%) → 0` over 1000ms, 80ms apart.
- **Rule:** scales X 0 → 1 over 1100ms after 160ms.
- **No opacity fades anywhere.**

### Lantern light

- A fixed 520px circle, `radial-gradient(closest-side, rgba(255,170,90,.16), rgba(255,140,70,.06) 55%, transparent)`, `mix-blend-mode: screen`.
- It follows the cursor with `1 − e^(−10·dt)` smoothing.
- It shows only on fine pointers.
